What this project can—and cannot—say

These are unadjusted, descriptive patterns in one survey sample. They are not causal findings and do not reproduce survey-weighted adjusted research. A depression symptom score is not a measure of every emotion; hs-CRP is one inflammation marker, not a measure of immune strength or recovery speed.

One dot, one participant

Each point below represents one complete adult NHANES participant. The horizontal position is their PHQ-9 symptom score; the vertical position is their hs-CRP concentration. Overlapping points are made visible with slight jitter and transparency. The vertical axis uses a log10 scale; hover over a point for age, sex, score, and concentration.

A marker, not a verdict

Inflammation is part of the body’s response to injury and infection. C-reactive protein is produced by the liver, and hs-CRP is a sensitive blood measure of this protein. It can be associated with many conditions and circumstances; one measurement does not describe someone’s whole immune system.

This plot shows how two measured values sit alongside one another in this sample. It cannot establish why either value is higher or lower, predict an individual’s health, or show that one causes the other.
